🔐 Privacy Policy
What We Collect
Your name, email, profile photo, messages, and MKJ number. Messages are stored on Firebase (Google servers).
How We Use It
Only to provide app functionality. Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.
Your MKJ Number
Your #MKJ number is permanent and uniquely assigned to your account. It cannot be changed or reassigned.
Data Storage
All data is stored securely on Firebase (Google Cloud). We use Firebase Authentication for secure login.
Your Rights
You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.
